Data Layer

Build the foundation for intelligent decisions.

The intelligence layer is only as strong as the operational data beneath it. We create structured, auditable data from the physical assets your organization operates every day.

Digital Asset Register

Replaces inefficient legacy systems such as scattered Excel sheets with a unified, digital registry for tracking organizational equipment.

  • Lifecycle status tracking — acquisition to disposal
  • Geospatial view of assets
  • Layered location hierarchy
  • Simplified ownership & accountability management
  • Purchase, warranty & valuation records
  • QR code & barcode tagging for movement

Services & Maintenance Mgt

Replaces the disjointed, untraceable emails + calls and paper that frustrates users and Facility, Maintenance and Service Managers.

  • Omnichannel request logging
  • Intelligent scheduling for routine servicing
  • From Requests to Execution to Reports — a complete audit trail
  • Keep service history of all equipment
  • Data-backed analytics of problematic assets and brands
  • Notifications on WhatsApp and email
